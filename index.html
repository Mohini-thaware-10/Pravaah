    From Application Submission to Accountable Review

    Understand the progression of an application through a structured sequence of administrative stages.

    1

    Application Submitted

    Institutional submits application with required documentation and complete forms.

    2

    Initial Scrutiny

    Administrative review verifies completeness, eligibility, and compliance with submission requirements.

    3

    Document Review

    Subject-matter experts review supporting documents, certifications, and eligibility evidence.

    4

    Compliance Review

    Authorized body validates regulatory adherence, statutory requirements, and policy compliance.

    5

    Final Decision

    Authorized authority communicates decision with conditions, next steps, and compliance obligations.

    Explore Workflow

    Application Management

    Organize, search, and track applications across all approval stages. Filter by status, department, submission date, and compliance status. Associate supporting documents and monitor progress in real time.

    Document Review

    Upload, organize, and review supporting documents alongside their associated application. Track review status, reviewer assignments, and evidence validity. Maintain a complete audit trail.

    Approval-to-Compliance Continuity

    Connect an approval decision to related obligations, supporting evidence, and deadlines. View the full chain from initial application through all post-approval requirements in one structured view.

    Deadline & Reporting

    Generate downloadable reports on application status, compliance obligations, and workflow summaries. View upcoming deadlines, overdue items, and required actions in a centralized dashboard.

    Greater Visibility

    Understand application progress and pending actions in one place. No more scattered emails, spreadsheets, or manual follow-ups.

    Better Traceability

    Connect review activity, evidence, and obligations across the full workflow. Every action is recorded, every decision is traceable.

    Structured Follow-up

    Identify approaching deadlines and actions requiring attention. Set priorities and ensure nothing falls through the cracks.

Official requirements, scrutiny, and approval decisions remain subject to the applicable process and authorized authorities.
