        PRAVAAH / Help & Guidelines

        Help & Guidelines

        Quick guidance for navigating the PRAVAAH demo workspace.

        Demo environment — guidance only. FRONTEND DEMONSTRATION

        Navigation Guide

        Key destinations available in this demo.

        • Overview/Approval Dashboard — KPIs, filters, attention items and activity (illustrative).
        • Applications — Browse, filter, sort, view details and manage demo actions.
        • Workflow Tracking — Pipeline visualization, stage details and bottlenecks.
        • Document Verification — Sample checklist and document preview.
        • Compliance Monitoring — Demo compliance register and review workspace.
        • Deadline & Alerts — Alerts, calendar and notification preferences.
        • Reports & Analytics — Charts, institution summary and report library with CSV export.
        • Approval Stages — Configure demo stages, reorder, preview workflow.
        • Audit Trail — Illustrative activity log with filters, details and CSV export.
        • User & Role Management — Demo users/roles and capability matrix.
        • Settings & Profile — Local preferences and fictional profile.

        Important Notes

        Scope and labeling reminders.

        • All data is local mock/illustrative data — not official AICTE statistics.
        • No backend, API, database, authentication or real integrations are implemented.
        • Actions like export/reset/save preferences affect only local demo state.
        • Compliance, roles and approvals shown are conceptual demonstrations.
